Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

In short: JobReady English works without an account. The developer does not collect data through the app, and your practice recording stays on your device.

Information on your device

The app stores language and career preferences, assessment results, practice progress, mistakes, bookmarks, goals, reminder preferences, checklist items, and saved STAR drafts locally to provide app features.

Microphone and recordings

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record. A practice recording is kept temporarily on your device for playback. It is not transcribed, analyzed, collected, or uploaded. A later recording may replace the previous recording.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If enabled, the app schedules a local reminder. You can disable reminders in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Collection and tracking

The current version does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, third-party sign-in, a remote database, or a user account system. The developer does not collect data through the app.

Purchases

Premium is offered as an Apple in-app purchase. Apple processes the transaction. JobReady English receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock Premium and does not receive your payment card details.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for employment and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The App Store listing will link to the current policy.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to uscivicspractice@gmail.com.

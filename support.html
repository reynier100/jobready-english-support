Change language

Restore Premium

Open Profile or the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Practice recordings

Recordings stay on your iPhone for private playback. They are not uploaded or analyzed, and a later recording may replace the previous one.

Daily reminders

Reminders are optional local notifications. Disable them at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.
